🌱 Who We Are

Driven by Compassion, Guided by Action

"We started Pajav Foundation not from boardrooms, but from the ground — from Sudhagad villages, from school corridors, from animal shelters. This is home."

Pajav Foundation is a registered Section 8 company working at the grassroots level across rural Maharashtra. Every rupee, every hour of volunteer time, goes directly to communities who need it most.

🎯
Our Mission
To bridge critical gaps in rural education, healthcare, and environmental care through direct, zero-middleman action.
🔭
Our Vision
A Maharashtra where every child learns, every family is healthy, and nature is respected and protected.
👥
Our Founders
Led by Ritesh V. Patil & Mayuri P. Modi — passionate grassroots change-makers from Raigad.
📋

Section 8 Company · 12A & 80G registration in progress · Darpan registered.

💪 What We Do

Our Four Core Initiatives

Four pillars. One mission: build a better tomorrow.

📚
Education Support

Stationery kits, scholarships, and learning camps for students in ZP and government schools across tribal regions.

🌊
Environmental Awareness

Beach cleaning drives, large-scale tree planting, and eco-awareness camps in coastal and tribal communities.

🏥
Health & Medical Camps

Free health checkups, medicine distribution, and mobile clinics reaching the most remote villages in Raigad.

🐄
Animal Welfare

Running Gaushalas, rescuing stray animals, and partnering with local municipal bodies for humane animal care.

Story of the Month
❤️ Human Stories

"I used to miss school because we couldn't afford notebooks. Now I have everything I need — and a scholarship too."

Ravi is 10 years old and lives in a small village in Sudhagad. His father works as a daily-wage labourer and his mother tends to their small farm. For two years, Ravi missed school more days than he attended — not because he didn't want to learn, but because his family couldn't afford basic stationery.

When Pajav Foundation's education drive reached his village, Ravi received a complete stationery kit and was enrolled in our scholarship programme. Today, he is among the top students in his class and dreams of becoming a doctor.

Ravi, Age 10
Sudhagad, Raigad · Education Programme Beneficiary

Recent Events & Drives

🔍 Full Transparency

How Your Money Actually Works

We follow a strict 3-step process to ensure complete accountability for every rupee donated.

1
Needs Assessment
We identify critical areas through direct community engagement, local leader surveys, and on-ground visits before any fund is touched.
2
Direct Implementation
Funds are deployed directly to projects with zero middlemen. 100% of field expenses are receipted and documented.
3
Audit & Reporting
We maintain transparent ledgers and undergo third-party financial audits. Reports are available to all donors on request.
